Assumption College of Davao (ACD) needs a more organized way to monitor Course Intended Learning Outcomes (CILOs) and Program / Graduate Outcomes (GOs), and to manage the evaluation evidence gathered from different stakeholder groups. Project CLOIE developed System CLOIE, a web-based academic evaluation and learning-outcome evidence system for organizing learning outcomes and their mappings, managing evaluation instruments and deployments, collecting stakeholder responses, and providing analytics and reports for academic review, quality assurance, continuous improvement, and accreditation preparation. The project followed an iterative Kanban-based development approach in which requirements and system functions were refined through client and adviser consultations, analysis of institutional evaluation instruments and academic documents, system reviews, implementation, and feedback. The implemented system supports evaluation workflows for students, graduating students, alumni, and industry partners, and applies authenticated access with role- and scope-based restrictions to academic and evaluation information. At the pre-final stage, the default automated test suite recorded 3,878 of 3,879 executed tests passing, with no failures, and a beta validation cycle involving 12 participants across five stakeholder groups produced 99 task-level ratings, of which 76.77% were rated Accepted. These results indicate a functioning implementation of the approved workflows, while database- and browser-level verification, full requirements traceability, and stakeholder acceptance records remain in progress. The project provides ACD with a centralized means of organizing, analyzing, and presenting learning-outcome and stakeholder evaluation evidence while keeping academic interpretation and decision-making with authorized institutional personnel. Final testing, validation, requirements traceability, and signed acceptance evidence will determine the extent to which the project objectives and success criteria are fully satisfied prior to Final Defense.
